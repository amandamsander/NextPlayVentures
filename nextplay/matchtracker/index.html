3. Per-player stats

Enter final totals for each player who played — leave a player at all zeros if they didn't play (they'll just be skipped). Goals are split by how they were scored; OT/PK is for a penalty shootout after the final whistle — those don't count toward on-field minutes or goals. Own goals (entered above) don't belong to any player — just make sure each on-field player's individual "GF" total still adds up to include them (see the checklist below).

4. Does it add up?

Regular-time on-field math: minutes should total the full game length × players on the field; goals-for/against should total (team goals scored/allowed, including any own goals) × players on the field for each one — same girls can count for more than one goal. OT/PK goals are tracked but excluded from these checks since no one's "on the field" for a shootout in the usual sense.
