About

Next Play Ventures LLC was started by a travel-sports parent who got tired of re-learning what to pack for every new tournament, sport, and season. We build practical, no-nonsense tools for the families living out of a duffel bag every weekend — players, parents, and coaches alike.

What we're building

Travel Sports Buddy

A smart, sport-specific packing checklist app for youth travel sports. Pick your sport, tell it who's packing and how you're getting there, and it builds the right list — every time.

Sport-specific packing, built for real tournaments

Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

This policy covers Next Play Ventures LLC's website and our app, Travel Sports Buddy, currently in development.

What we collect

When you sign in to Travel Sports Buddy, we collect your name and email address from your Google or Apple account, so you can access your own trips and packing lists. We store the trips, packing lists, and custom items you create so you can access them across sessions and share them with people you invite.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information. We don't share your trip or packing data with anyone except the people you specifically invite to a shared trip.

Advertising

Travel Sports Buddy plans to show ads through Google AdMob and may include Amazon affiliate links on relevant products. These services may collect limited technical information (like device type) as described in Google's and Amazon's own privacy policies.

Questions

Reach out any time at amanda.sander@nextplayventures.org. This policy will be expanded and finalized before Travel Sports Buddy's public launch.
